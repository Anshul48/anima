# Vector 5 evidence note: creative coverage under compression

**Status:** bounded primary-source inspection, 2026-10-06. All computational implications below are proposals for architecture discussion. No mechanism is implemented, experiment initiated, or recommendation ratified. This note follows [CREATIVITY-AND-CONSTRAINTS.md](CREATIVITY-AND-CONSTRAINTS.md), which was read first. It replaces no existing responsibility or accepted task commitment.

This is a companion to [Vector 5 — Creative coverage under compression](VECTOR-05-CREATIVE-COVERAGE.md). The user's concern is that compact summaries can blur unusual evidence, while connections among material in different holons may enable useful insight. Neuroscience offers leads about constructing possibilities from memory, applying structure to new material, and evaluating ideas. It does not directly establish how TRACE should compress its records or preserve discovery opportunities. The four studies below were chosen for accessible primary text and distinct tasks, including two interventions. They are not a comprehensive creativity survey.

Two preservation questions should remain distinct: whether attributable material survives somewhere, and whether a later inquiry can find and use the distinction absent from its summary. Neither success proves the other. The proposed target is economical navigation with recoverable exceptions, alternative interpretations and candidate connections. It is not literal biological forgetting or an anatomical sequence prescribed for software.

## 1. Retrieval orientation can change divergent output

**Primary study:** Madore, Addis and Schacter, *Creativity and Memory: Effects of an Episodic Specificity Induction on Divergent Thinking*, Psychological Science 26, 1461–1468, September 2015; [journal DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/0956797615591863). Inspected the [primary author-manuscript copy](https://moodle2.units.it/pluginfile.php/744549/mod_resource/content/5/Madore%2C%202015_Creativity%20and%20Memory-%20Effects%20of%20an%20Episodic%20Specificity%20Induction%20on%20Divergent%20Thinking.pdf).

**Inspection, 2026-10-06:** abstract; both experiments' participants, inductions, tasks, scoring and results; general discussion. Separate supplementary scripts and analyses were not inspected.

**Finding and limit:** In two within-subject experiments, each retaining 23 young adults, brief detail-focused recollection training increased categories of appropriate alternate object uses relative to controls. Future-imagination detail served as a manipulation check. Object association showed no comparable gain; the convergent-task contrast required caution because its interaction was marginal. This supports a task-specific intervention effect, not proof that more detail improves every creative process or originality per idea. It does not isolate a neural circuit or manipulate summary compression.

**Proposed implication:** compare detail-oriented retrieval with ordinary summary access when generating candidates.

## 2. Replay can reorganize experience under learned structure

**Primary study:** Liu, Dolan, Kurth-Nelson and Behrens, *Human Replay Spontaneously Reorganizes Experience*, Cell, 2019-07-25; [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2019.06.012). Inspected the [publisher-formatted primary PDF deposited at UCL](https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/10078375/1/1-s2.0-S0092867419306403-main.pdf).

**Inspection, 2026-10-06:** abstract; main task description and inferred-sequence findings; selected discussion; STAR+Methods participant and task sections. Raw MEG data, analysis code and the complete supplementary analysis were not independently examined.

**Finding and limit:** Two studies analysed 21 and 22 participants. Participants learned a structural rule and encountered new objects in scrambled order. Resting reactivation followed inferred rather than presented order; abstract sequence and position representations accompanied object representations. This supports structure-guided reorganization in an explicitly taught task. MEG decoding is indirect evidence, not direct identification of every hippocampal event. The task supplies a correct structure; it does not demonstrate scientific discovery or a universally creative replay mechanism.

**Proposed implication:** explore candidate relationships constrained by alternative explicit structures, rather than replaying records verbatim.

## 3. Generation and evaluation are distinguishable but interacting

**Primary study:** Ellamil, Dobson, Beeman and Christoff, *Evaluative and Generative Modes of Thought During the Creative Process*, NeuroImage 59, 1783–1794, 2012; available online 2011-08-11. Inspected the [author-hosted published paper](https://www.christofflab.ca/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/Ellamil_2012_Neuroimage.pdf); [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuroimage.2011.08.008).

**Inspection, 2026-10-06:** abstract; participants and drawing-task procedure; main activation and connectivity results; selected discussion and stated interpretive limitations. No imaging data or statistical reproduction.

**Finding and limit:** Fifteen art/design students alternated instructed generation and evaluation while designing book covers. Generation preferentially recruited medial temporal regions; evaluation recruited executive and default-network regions, with positive connectivity across task performance. These are task-conditioned fMRI associations. The imposed alternation does not establish a natural universal sequence, and block measurements cannot resolve every brief internal process or prove simultaneous participation. Self-ratings concerned separating the instructed modes, not independent certification of product usefulness.

**Proposed implication:** let construction and assessment interact without assuming one anatomical module exclusively generates or evaluates ideas.

## 4. Perturbation separates originality from other output measures

**Primary study:** Bartoli and colleagues, *Default Mode Network Electrophysiological Dynamics and Causal Role in Creative Thinking*, Brain 147, 3409–3425; published online 2024-06-18. Inspected the [primary full article in PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11449134/); [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1093/brain/awae199).

**Inspection, 2026-10-06:** participant and task methods; stimulation design; behavioural scoring; stimulation results; limitations. Supplementary controls, raw recordings and code were not reproduced.

**Finding and limit:** Thirteen epilepsy patients were recorded; nine were stimulated at selected default-network sites. Stimulation reduced semantic-distance originality scores in alternate uses, without detected changes in variability, category flexibility or mind-wandering scores. Perturbation supports contribution to this task. Clinically constrained sampling, mixed sites and limited sham/control-site options restrict inference. Semantic distance is not verified usefulness or scientific novelty, and nonsignificant differences do not prove absence of effects.

**Proposed implication:** assess valid usefulness, originality, coverage and output quantity separately.

## What the combined evidence can justify

The defensible inspiration is functional: recover specific material, construct relationships under usable structures, and assess candidates using several criteria. The evidence does not select a software topology, retention policy or optimal exploration budget. In particular, it does not establish the old proposed CA3-to-ripple-to-mPFC cascade, nor prove that constraint removal is the source of creativity. Learned structure can guide construction; an unsuitable structure can also narrow it. Which structures help a computational inquiry remains a question for that inquiry.

Imagination constructs a possible event or scenario; divergent thinking produces several possible responses; evaluation judges candidates against criteria; general discovery requires establishing something consequentially new and supported in a domain. Those functions can interact, but gains on one should not be reported as gains on all. Producing more valid alternatives is useful even when it does not improve the best answer. Producing a surprising answer is insufficient when its apparent novelty comes from error.

None of the selected studies directly measures rare-record survival through repeated summarization, successful cross-holon evidence retrieval, or validated discovery in an autonomous knowledge system. Keeping those engineering questions explicit preserves the ambition without borrowing an empirical guarantee from biological findings.

## Proposed compression and construction mechanisms

A summary could serve as an entry point rather than the entire meaning of a region. Boundary cases, conditions, competing explanations and unresolved residuals could remain attributable and independently retrievable. A later query may need an item that is peripheral to the region's ordinary description. An exception route should therefore be judged by whether it recovers the relevant distinction, not merely by whether the original file still exists.

Candidate construction could use several organizations of the same evidence: causal conditions, functional roles, temporal dependencies, counterexamples or analogy. Cross-holon links would begin as proposals. Similarity can suggest a connection without establishing support, identity or causation. Distinct expert interpretations could remain available when a common summary would otherwise collapse them; preserving differences does not require preserving unsupported endorsement.

Computational replay could mean revisiting selected episodes or candidate trajectories under changed assumptions. It should expose which elements were retrieved, recombined or inferred and which consequences remain unchecked. The useful test is whether this revisiting changes a decision, finds a missing condition or produces a better supported candidate. More replay events, messages or generated tokens are not themselves improvement.

For a hypothetical example, one holon contains mostly successful procedures plus a failure under an unusual condition. Another organizes tasks where that condition frequently occurs. Their common summaries may offer no useful bridge. Recovering the exception could suggest a new experiment or design adaptation. It could also expose a measurement error rather than a new mechanism. Both outcomes can improve inquiry; automatic promotion of the anomaly would confuse preservation with validation.

Constraint-aware generation should distinguish learned schema preferences from sourced commitments and checkable conditions. An exploratory branch can vary a proposed-world assumption while its real resource use and actions remain governed by applicable obligations. Evaluation can guide another construction pass. Protected access to unusual candidates should permit investigation without making every candidate active indefinitely or turning a judgment of unfamiliarity into a prohibition.

## Less anatomical alternatives and discriminating questions

Ordinary source retrieval with overlapping indexes, query expansion and explicit counterexample searches may provide the needed coverage. A concise case library with recoverable preconditions may beat a complex replay system. Structured analogy, exhaustive search in a bounded space, or a capable model using carefully selected evidence may solve the same construction problem. These are meaningful alternatives when given comparable sources, context opportunities and complete budgets.

Three questions could distinguish useful mechanisms without starting experiments here:

1. Does summary navigation plus residual or source access recover consequential exceptions better than equally resourced direct retrieval, including after repeated compression and changed queries?
2. Does revisiting and recombining evidence improve the quality of the selected candidate beyond ordinary generation with equivalent evidence and tokens? Separate diversity, validity, usefulness and cost.
3. Do several schemas or persistent interpretations improve held-out transfer when the dominant organization is misleading, while remaining revisable through contradictory evidence?

Coverage should include omitted conditions, noisy anomalies and candidates that are unfamiliar but valid. Planted cases can expose mechanics; real domain evidence is needed for discovery claims. Complete cost includes retrieval, maintenance of alternative views, assessment, failed exploration and reconstruction. Better performance may justify those costs, but anatomical resemblance cannot.

## Access and claim boundaries

Four primary studies were selected. PMC openings for Madore and Liu encountered browser challenges; complete primary-text copies were then obtained from an institutional teaching repository and UCL, respectively. Ellamil's author-hosted paper and Bartoli's PMC article were accessible. Full text availability is distinct from the selected inspection scopes above. No source was experimentally reproduced; no secondary commentary was used as empirical evidence. Publication and inspection dates are distinguished.

Only the earlier creativity dossier and applicable local instruction locations were read for workspace context. No memory registry, private chats, genesis B files, old 4C project, canonical context or project backlog was accessed or changed. This is a bounded evidence note for Vector 5 architecture, not source reproduction, implementation qualification or user acceptance.

# Independent review of the integrated architecture packet

**Status:** Independent documentation review, 2026-10-05. References identify the inspected draft and its line numbers. This is not empirical qualification, architecture acceptance, or implementation authorization.

**Verdict:** No critical or high-severity fault found. The synthesis preserves organizational transformation, fusion/fission, state custody, lifecycle, inheritance, physical development, and methodological diversity. It distinguishes context salience from commitments and schema preferences, acknowledges outlier loss, and makes no society-to-AGI guarantee. The following medium gaps should be clarified before the discussion map is treated as an architectural baseline.

## Medium: evaluator development can change the meaning of improvement

**References:** [SYNTHESIS.md](SYNTHESIS.md), lines 33, 61–70, 195; [RESPONSIBILITIES.md](../../RESPONSIBILITIES.md), lines 14, 24–29, 38.

The recurring-cooperation example includes a revised evaluator, and assessment is itself an adaptation surface. That is appropriate in a developmental system, but the packet does not explicitly distinguish improving observation or measurement from changing the objective or acceptance semantics. A composition could otherwise appear better because it changed what success means.

**Recommended amendment:** State that an evaluator revision carries its own evidence and a description of which claims remain comparable. Separate measurement repairs from changes to governing objectives or accepted requirements. Where comparability is lost, retain that limitation rather than reporting a continuous improvement series. Routine measurement repair can remain within standing delegation; this does not imply another human approval for every change.

**Unresolved tradeoff:** A permanently frozen evaluator can block useful development, while unrestricted evaluator adaptation permits moving targets. The architecture needs attributable revision without granting a participant unilateral power to redefine the claim it is proving.

## Medium: inheritance needs continuity of conditional meaning

**References:** [SYNTHESIS.md](SYNTHESIS.md), lines 67–68, 82, 163–167, 203, 218; [CONSTRUCTION-LANGUAGES.md](CONSTRUCTION-LANGUAGES.md), lines 55, 99, 107.

The drafts preserve lineage, obligations, applicability, and retirement, but the synthesis does not explicitly name compositional forgetting. A procedure may be exported, incorporated into a composite, and inherited again; each summary can retain the successful action while dropping the condition or failed attempt that bounded its validity. Cold source recovery does not ensure that the descendant will know what to retrieve.

**Recommended amendment:** Add that inherited capabilities may require preconditions, counterexamples, dependencies, interpretive context, and continued availability of referenced evidence. This is a minimum relevant context question, not a requirement to copy every transcript. Vector 8 should examine what a successor must carry, can reconstruct, or must reacquire.

**Unresolved tradeoff:** Keeping complete histories multiplies storage and interpretation costs. Thin exports reduce cost but can make later reuse overconfident or impossible to reassess. The packet should preserve both possibilities and compare their effects on downstream reuse.

## Medium: vector ownership shorthand can misroute the work

**References:** [SYNTHESIS.md](SYNTHESIS.md), lines 187, 215, 217; [OPERATIVE-BRIEF.md](../../../context/OPERATIVE-BRIEF.md), lines 19–23, 34; [RESPONSIBILITIES.md](../../RESPONSIBILITIES.md), lines 11–16, 38.

The prose correctly retains TL's source-organization responsibilities. The vector table nevertheless assigns creative coverage to TRACE + SST, despite candidate mechanisms involving source access, memberships, and navigation projections. It assigns programme search to SST + STC, although a large organization's authoritative workflow state belongs to its responsible domain world and existing operational systems. STC is not automatically the executor or custodian of every organizational process.

**Recommended amendment:** Include TL where creative-coverage research changes source organization or retrieval. Describe programme search as domain-world responsibility with SST, using STC where construction or execution is actually within its accepted remit. Mark “Main home” as coordination responsibility for the question, not an implicit transfer of custody.

**Unresolved tradeoff:** Shared machinery can reduce duplicate work, but centralizing a domain's state model can also erase local semantics. The graph-building capability remains a candidate responsibility with an owner to determine.

## Low: the eight vectors guide discussion more clearly than decisions

**References:** [SYNTHESIS.md](SYNTHESIS.md), lines 209–222.

The vectors are coherent and cover the supplied directions. Several outputs are vocabularies, criteria, or research comparisons, however, so completing those outputs could leave the consequential architecture choice untouched.

**Recommended amendment:** For each vector when it is discussed, name a plausible competing arrangement and the observation that would favor one. Examples include separate expert worlds versus internal specialists with distinct overlays; shared evidence references versus duplicated inherited context; and common transition machinery versus world-owned transitions. Keep the sequence revisable. These discriminators should not become eight mandatory gates or a prerequisite for current module releases.

## Followup: framing actual development mechanisms

The lead subsequently proposed executable organizational transformations, locally learning world/relationship policies, and inherited developmental rules that generate organization. This addresses a gap in the inspected draft: a vocabulary of adaptation surfaces and retained accounts describes change without necessarily supplying mechanisms that produce useful development.

The three proposals answer different questions. Executable transformations define what change is possible and its state effects. Learning policies choose or adapt changes using observations and feedback. Inherited rules provide a generative prior or process across lifetimes. They can coexist; they are not three interchangeable candidates for one architectural slot.

Useful discriminators are where transformation power lives, what signals change a policy, and what inheritance carries. A world-owned change procedure and a shared transition executor are competing allocations. Prediction-error learning and task-outcome learning can produce different behavior. Inheriting a successful organization, conditional procedures, or a generator of organizations poses different generalization and recovery problems.

The next framing should identify a concrete organizational effect each mechanism could produce and which simpler arrangement lacks it. A rule that generates organization needs conditions, available operations, resource consequences, and ways to respond to failed outcomes. Accurate local prediction can coexist with poor task performance. A copied developmental rule can also inherit an unsuitable bias. Local learning, development within a lifetime, and improvement across descendants should retain separate claims.

This clarification should remain a set of hypotheses, with mechanisms allowed to coexist. It should not prescribe one reward, universal prediction-error algorithm, developmental grammar, or approval ceremony across worlds. The current packet has not empirically discriminated these options.

## Competing architectures worth keeping live

**A small lifecycle substrate with world-owned development.** The host supplies resources, identities, placement, and lifecycle; worlds implement representation change, integration, and inheritance using domain-specific mechanisms. Shared meaning begins with exported effects and responsibilities. This preserves local expressiveness and uses existing primitives, but makes cross-world transformations harder to coordinate and compare. The synthesis's shared adaptation vocabulary is useful under this alternative without requiring one universal change executor.

**One hierarchical knowledge environment with differentiated trajectories.** TL provides shared versioned source organization; one TRACE maintains separate interpretive roles, methods, and working histories, while SST supports task-specific search. Federation or extraction follows a demonstrated need for stronger independence. This may reduce duplication and consultation costs, but could inadequately protect divergent salience and long-lived disagreements. Persistent separate worlds remain a real alternative. Neither arrangement has been empirically favored by this packet.

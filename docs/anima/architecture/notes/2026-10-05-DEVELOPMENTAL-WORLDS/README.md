My central proposal is to organize the next discussion around **developmental changes and their consequences**. A world can revise a representation, procedure, relationship, participant, organization or embodiment. The architecture should keep the affected state, responsibilities, applicable commitments and evidence intelligible through the revision. Making change possible and discovering changes that help are separate capabilities.

This framing preserves the full substrate ambition: co-adaptation, assimilation, fusion, fission, composites and inheritance, alongside useful local adaptation. It also permits hardware work, persistent expert societies and creative search to develop on different timescales.

## Discussion sequence

**Vector 1 asks what counts as a developmental change.**

We can test the answer on a few transformations: an expert coalition becomes a persistent composite; a familiar construction workflow acquires a new representation; a physical subsystem changes its morphology and control together. For each, identify what changes, which obligations remain, who can enact the change and what would show it helped.

**Vector 2 asks how selective attention can coexist with continuity and revision.** It distinguishes context selection, belief updates, scoped commitments and effect checks.

**Vector 3 asks when expert individuality is useful and how it can develop.** It considers differences within a hierarchical TRACE as well as across worlds, and treats relationships and composites as possible holders of expertise.

**Vector 4 asks what the physical construction environment should provide.** It connects conceptual design to engineering realization and interpretable feedback, with a small software entry path and parallel hardware work. Detailed actuation research can proceed separately.

**Vector 5 asks how useful outliers and unfamiliar combinations can influence work under compression.** It distinguishes discovery bottlenecks, permits routes beyond current summaries, and considers how investigation can develop representations, procedures and relationships.

**Vector 6 asks how experience can improve what the system constructs and how it constructs it.** Representations, executable operations and selection guidance can develop together. Typed choices, library compression, actual effects and prospective reuse warrant separate claims.

**Vector 7 asks how alternatives relate to the conditions and consequences of a larger body of work.** Decision views, joint contributions, shared resources, evidence and actual execution remain distinguishable. Search can propose changes to formulation and cooperation as well as select continuations.

**Vector 8 asks what advantage survives into further work.** Conditional competence, developmental methods, relationships and prepared environments can be inherited in different forms. Active retirement, historical preservation, ongoing duties and reconstruction remain separate responsibilities. The eight-vector round is now ready for the user's response; its proposals remain provisional.

# Primary-source seeds for SOP research

Prepared 2026-10-08. This is a small starting set for the cheaper research assignment, not deep research, a validated SOP suite or an adoption decision.

## Sources inspected in preparation

- [Michael Nygard: Documenting Architecture Decisions](https://cognitect.com/blog/2011/11/15/documenting-architecture-decisions), 2011-11-15. Describes concise records of consequential choices, context, status and consequences; retains superseded decisions. Relevant to reasons and revision history. Its experience report does not establish that one format is optimal here.
- [Rust RFC process](https://github.com/rust-lang/rfcs/blob/main/README.md), accessed 2026-10-08. Separates ordinary changes from substantial proposals and describes preparation, review, disposition and implementation. Acceptance does not itself assign implementation priority or a developer. Relevant to distinguishing idea, decision, assignment and delivery; its community governance and waiting periods need not transfer.
- [Google engineering practices: The Standard of Code Review](https://google.github.io/eng-practices/review/reviewer/standard.html), accessed 2026-10-08. Discusses progress versus perfection, minor comments, technical grounds and resolving disagreement. Relevant to proportionate review and avoiding stalls; it is code-review guidance rather than a complete research or architecture method.

These are primary practice descriptions. Recommendations and reported experience are not comparative proof that our adapted SOPs improve outcomes.

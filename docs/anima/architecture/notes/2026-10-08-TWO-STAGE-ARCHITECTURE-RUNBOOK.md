## 1. Curate and integrate

Open a capable cheaper agent in that directory. Paste the complete [curator prompt](2026-10-08-CONTEXT-INTEGRATION-PROMPT.md).

It reads the identified conversation and existing documents, preserves distinct substantive wording, organizes scope and provenance, synthesizes the material and checks coverage. It also handles only the expressly conditional legacy ecosystem cleanup.

Its stable output is **ANIMA/START-HERE.md**, leading to **ANIMA/context/integrations/2026-10-08/ARCHITECTURE-HANDOFF.md**, synthesis, precise source routes and the coverage record.

Let the curator finish and report the actual capture frontier and gaps. Complete B1/B2 review remains separately paused. Missing future research does not by itself prevent a documentary handoff.

## 2. Propose the architecture

Start a **fresh session** in the same directory after the curator delivers a usable handoff. Paste the complete [architect prompt](2026-10-08-ARCHITECTURE-AUTHORING-PROMPT.md).

The architect starts from the stable entry point, checks relevant contracts and evidence, selects and challenges a coherent design, and prepares a first realization and proposed coordinator handoff.

Its proposed packet goes under **ANIMA/architecture/proposals/2026-10-08-DEVELOPMENTAL-SUBSTRATE/**, or a linked revision directory if a completed packet already exists.

It must also produce complete shared SOPs and ready-to-paste project handoffs, then issue them through authorized internal channels or record specific remaining delivery dependencies. Existing coordination handles distribution/adoption tracking within its role. The user does not adapt a template separately for each project.

This stage requires architectural judgment, rather than another condensation pass. The user does not need to review every exploratory essay before it begins. Unresolved governing choices remain explicit and require input before dependent decisions can be accepted.

## What follows

**Preserve and integrate → design and challenge → review consequential choices and accept a baseline → authorize a coordinator to build and verify.**

No builder or coordinator starts automatically from these prompts. Current project delivery continues under its existing authority.

Macro architecture and integrated context belong in ANIMA; project-local architecture, source code and delivery records stay with their owners. The architect may propose a source home for genuinely new runtime machinery, but does not create or select it by writing documents.

My recommendation is to make the personal assistant the first demanding application of a developmental substrate. Use existing execution machinery for the initial assistant; give it persistent experience and an executable capability repertoire; develop its ability to select, adapt, compose, and retain useful capabilities through real work. STC contributes software construction. STC-Lite remains an assurance profile within STC. The general substrate supplies continuity and facilities for organizational change, with its value assessed against a capable simpler arrangement.

This preserves both ambitions: an assistant that makes the user's life and research easier, and a computational environment in which increasingly capable organization can develop. They can inform each other without making every possible substrate mechanism a prerequisite for useful assistance.

The direct user direction is a highly capable personal assistant that learns and acts on their behalf, openness to divergent mechanisms, biological inspiration, continuity of crucial state, and escape from tool-management paralysis. In this conversation the user clarified: research-to-experiment-to-artifact and software construction are both goals, with software construction the immediate priority. That selects the first domain, without accepting the proposed architecture or authorizing implementation. B1/B2 are supplied conceptual syntheses, with prior-model prose and schematic mathematics. Their broader thesis deserves investigation; their particular equations, biological interpretations, and implementation prescriptions do not become commitments merely because they appear in the context.

The existing [operative brief](../../context/OPERATIVE-BRIEF.md) preserves A → C → B → D: computationally useful knowledge; learned schemas and operations; cumulative capability discovery; computational and physical co-design. This is a priority map. Useful learning and a genuinely difficult structural experiment can begin before a complete ecosystem exists. Hardware preparation can proceed alongside software work when it answers a real research need.

The most valuable shift in B1/B2 is that a capability can change its place in the system. An improvised operation can become a reliable skill; several skills can become a reusable procedure; a procedure can acquire its own state and execution responsibility; a repeatedly valuable composite can become shared infrastructure. Integration can also remove redundant mechanisms, redistribute state, specialize participants, or permit a useful split. This includes changes to participants themselves, beyond adjusting connections between fixed plugins.

Many ingredients were already present in [SUBSTRATE-DIRECTION.md](../../../../components/substrate/docs/SUBSTRATE-DIRECTION.md). B1/B2 sharpen developmental rules, overlapping functional participation, the distinction between adaptation and inheritance, and the possibility that successful computation becomes part of the substrate. That last idea can have a concrete software interpretation today: a repeatedly reconstructed model-mediated operation becomes a checked executable implementation. Specialized hardware is a later possible implementation, selected using measured workload and resource costs.

Your three-box example gives a useful rule for choosing the scale of change. If two trips solve the problem, change the action sequence. If a carrying method repeatedly fails, adapt the procedure. If the existing repertoire cannot achieve the required effect, construct a new capability. If recurring coordination between capabilities dominates the work, investigate changing their organization. These alternatives can coexist; novelty does not automatically require a new persistent component.

Biology helps pose these questions, but does not supply a ready-made selection policy for generated software. Tools have versioned dependencies, side effects, and applicability conditions. A useful engineering policy must learn which of these matter for the task, observe failures, and preserve the evidence needed to revise its choices. We can implement a useful provisional policy now and improve it through experience. Its incompleteness should not block the assistant.

Several claims in the condensations need restraint. A UUID records identity; it does not demonstrate a self-maintaining organization. Mutual information can reflect copying or a shared input, so maximizing it does not establish useful integration. A connection-strength scalar cannot represent semantic compatibility, authority, ownership, or distinct information types. The gradient expressions are illustrations until parameters, feedback, and an actual optimization mechanism exist. Variation and selection can preserve poor local solutions or produce bloat; neither increasing complexity nor increasing capability follows automatically. Physical constraints can encourage useful organization, but hardware alone cannot determine which objectives or authorities the assistant should respect.

There are three plausible ways forward:

| Choice | What it buys | What it costs or leaves open |
|---|---|---|
| Expand STC into the general assistant | Fast access to an existing construction workflow | Software-delivery assumptions can dominate personal assistance and general organization; the Lite profile does not solve this distinction |
| Complete a general developmental host first | A coherent environment for broad transformation experiments | Delays useful assistance and risks implementing mechanisms whose practical value has not been established |
| Build the assistant with a small shared foundation and add developmental mechanisms where tasks demand them | Immediate usefulness, direct feedback, and a research path grounded in changing tasks | Requires honest boundaries between ordinary orchestration, learned capability selection, and demonstrated structural development |

I recommend the third choice. Conceptually, the substrate is general and STC is one participating construction world. Implementation can begin as one assistant process, existing tools, a durable store, and selected substrate services. Those conceptual responsibilities do not require four independently deployed layers, a new central coordinator, or immediate adoption of the alpha host for every task. Independent modules can remain independently buildable and later participate through deliberate pins and actual-caller qualification.

The assistant needs continuity of obligations, useful memory, dependable action, and evidence about its own competence. Its personal model should distinguish explicit user direction from inferred habits. It should prepare useful work under standing authorization, recover after interruption, and ask about consequential semantic branches. Initiative earns its place through progress and reduced human effort; more suggestions and more activity are not adequate measures. These responsibilities can initially use existing mechanisms. TRACE/TL, SST, Reflex, or Entorhinal become integrations when their actual contracts and qualification justify them, without forcing the assistant to wait for the whole dependency graph.

For reuse versus creation, make the decision over a plan and its expected consequences. The repertoire should include invoking an existing capability, changing parameters, composing capabilities, adapting an implementation or representation, constructing a new capability, and seeking missing direction when the task requires it. First determine which alternatives are authorized and semantically applicable. Then compare expected task quality, uncertainty, total execution cost, recovery cost, and likely future maintenance. An estimate can guide a cheap trial; it should not be presented as a proven optimal policy.

A small provisional procedure is enough:

1. Retrieve relevant capabilities using the desired effect, task context, and known failure conditions. Inspect dependencies, versions, and applicability for the leading candidates.
2. Use an adequate existing plan when its expected quality and total cost justify it. Permit adaptation or fresh construction immediately when evidence makes those alternatives preferable.
3. Check the actual result using an assessment appropriate to the task. Preserve failures and distinguish tool failure, evaluator defect, changed requirements, and missing user direction.
4. Store the episode automatically. Treat an improvised tool as local to the episode initially; retain it for broader reuse when there is evidence of applicability, recurring value, or costly reconstruction.
5. On later tasks, revise both the capability and the policy that chooses it. Retire active entries or merge duplicates when maintaining them is costly, while retaining required provenance and reconstructable artifacts.

Frequency alone is insufficient: a rarely used recovery capability can be essential. Repeated retrieval failure can also justify improving descriptions or the selector before creating another equivalent tool. Conversely, a capability that is expensive to rediscover may be worth retaining after one use. Background consolidation should carry its own measured cost; an ever-growing unreviewed catalog recreates the original problem.

The minimum useful record is modest: desired effect; applicability conditions; input/output meaning; executable artifact and dependency identity; assessed examples and failures; provenance; and measured costs. A retained construction recipe may explain how to rebuild or adapt the capability. Start with an ordinary store and bounded retrieval. Add graphs, learned indexes, or abstraction discovery only where their effect on successful work can be assessed. The user should not have to name, file, or classify every new tool.

Forgetting should primarily mean reducing active attention and retiring unnecessary executable arrangements. Important obligations need their own persistent state and enforcement where applicable. Working context can remain small while detailed evidence and cold capabilities remain recoverable. A model's failure to recall an instruction should not create permission to bypass an enforced resource or access boundary. Operational restrictions can be enforced today, while understanding values, intent, and legitimate revision remains a harder semantic problem.

The current substrate procedure executor explicitly assumes caller-trusted procedures; [its consumer contract](../../../../components/substrate/docs/CONSUMER.md) describes filesystem and network restrictions that are contractual rather than enforced. If the assistant generates executable tools, its execution environment must supply the actual confinement promised by the assistant's mandate. That is an integration requirement, not a capability established by the alpha package's ledger or by calling an interface a membrane.

Structural development should have a concrete before/after consequence. Suppose collecting experiment results, normalizing them, and generating a report repeatedly requires three participants to coordinate over shared task state. A developmental candidate could consolidate the shared state, remove duplicated parsing, specialize one operation, transfer relevant responsibility, and export a reusable experiment-report capability. A split should partition the necessary state and obligations where the contract supports it. The change deserves retention only when outcomes, recovery, cost, or human effort justify it. A new wrapper and a new name can be useful composition without establishing the stronger integration claim.

Retained finished capabilities and retained developmental procedures are different things. A descendant that runs an old program unchanged demonstrates reuse. A descendant that inherits an effective way to construct a suitable capability for an unfamiliar task demonstrates something stronger. We should record these separately, together with within-run adaptation, across-run learning, structural development, and population-level evolution. Model weights can remain frozen while external capabilities and policies improve; this does not imply that the model's internal learning machinery has changed.

Following the user's clarification, use software construction as the first demand, with research feeding into architecture and implementation when useful. The first journey should run from a real repository objective through necessary investigation, architectural decisions, construction, independent checks, and a reviewable artifact. The proposals below produce observable outcomes while preserving research as the other major target:

1. Given a software objective and an authorized repository, investigate the requirements, surface consequential architectural choices, implement the bounded feature or fix under the accepted baseline, and produce a useful artifact with independent integrated checks. Include research or a discriminating experiment where it resolves a real implementation uncertainty.
2. Give a related repository task in a fresh session. The assistant should recover relevant decisions, known failed approaches, and useful construction capabilities without asking the user to reconstruct them or manually organize the tools.
3. Introduce a held-out requirement or environment change that genuinely breaks a prior assumption: changed input semantics, a different data representation, or an incompatible dependency. Keep the user's required behavior explicit and independently checkable.
4. Let the assistant detect the mismatch, choose a suitable response, check it, and retain useful evidence and mechanisms. Then test on another related but unfamiliar task.
5. Instantiate a fresh assistant state with only the permitted retained repertoire or developmental recipe. Measure which advantage survives, and account for the work needed to produce it.

This is a proposed experiment, not permission to launch it. The first architectural acceptance should specify the selected journey, its domain boundary, assessment method, and acceptable resource envelope. The existing deferred learning and adaptation experiments do not become authorized merely because this note recommends a new test.

The comparison must be difficult enough to expose a benefit. Use the same underlying model and comparable total resources. Give the capable baseline persistent notes, tool discovery, code generation, and ordinary code repair; it must not be artificially denied adaptation. Compare that with a condition retaining structured executable capabilities and selection evidence. Add structural reorganization as a further condition only when the task exposes a plausible coordination or representation bottleneck. A simple capability library may win, and that would be useful evidence about where the larger substrate is needed.

Assess task correctness and usefulness first. Also measure human corrections and management time, repeated mistakes, stale or inapplicable reuse, recovery under interruption, total inference and background consolidation cost, and held-out transfer. Keep required outcomes separate from costs so cheap failures do not score as success. Validate that the changed-condition task actually distinguishes an unchanged solution from a suitable repair before trusting its adaptation verdict. Preserve all failed and VOID legs.

Where objective checks exist, use independent execution or inspection. Where the issue is the quality of a theoretical insight, human or appropriate domain-expert judgment remains part of the oracle. That does not prevent progress on reproducibility, arithmetic, source fidelity, or executable behavior. A second agent's agreement supplies a different opinion; it cannot manufacture absent ground truth. Expensive discovery should be aimed at uncertainties that the available evidence can resolve.

Current prior art helps locate the research contribution. [Voyager](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.16291) combines executable skill memory, retrieval, and environmental feedback in Minecraft. [DreamCoder](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.08381) jointly grows symbolic abstractions and learns guidance for program search. Their domains and machinery differ from this assistant; together they suggest investigating the repertoire and its selector as interacting learning problems. That last connection is an inference for this proposal, not a reported result about ANIMA.

[Darwin Gödel Machine](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.22954) explores and evaluates code-modified coding agents in an archive. The newer [Hyperagents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.19461) makes the improvement mechanism editable and reports transfer of meta-level improvements across domains. These are relevant to the later B frontier; their results do not establish universal open-ended intelligence or justify making recursive self-improvement a prerequisite for the first assistant.

The [Hermes Agent repository](https://github.com/NousResearch/hermes-agent) currently describes persistent memory and skills created and revised through use. This is a contemporary adjacent reference, not an identification of the exact “Hermes Plus” variant mentioned by the user, nor an independent evaluation of its performance. A generic skill-saving feature would therefore be a weak differentiation claim. [Cordis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25512) and the [DSH primer](https://deepseek-harness.github.io/deepseek-harness/en/reference/cordis-primer) supply composition, dependency resolution, events, and effect lifecycle machinery. These references help identify reusable mechanisms; they do not report that DSH autonomously discovers beneficial organizational transitions.

The more interesting research hypothesis is that jointly developing representations, executable capabilities, selection policies, and organizational structure produces transferable useful work with less human management and better total cost than competent conventional assistance. Names such as holon, membrane, and ecosystem do not establish novelty. An unfamiliar task completed using an inherited organizational improvement would be meaningful evidence; demonstrating it across substantially different task families would support a broader claim.

Keep several mechanisms open. Conditional skill memory may solve much of the immediate pain. Program-library learning and compilation may improve how the assistant constructs and reuses operations. Persistent specialist participants may help when distinct experience and local state reduce central coordination. Deeper integration or developmental recipes may matter when repeated work reveals a shared representation or coordination bottleneck. Hardware specialization becomes attractive when a measured computational bottleneck supports it. None of these hypotheses needs to monopolize the architecture before a discriminating task exists.

The concrete next artifact should be a bounded software-assistant capability contract: one real repository journey, what it must remember across restarts, which routine actions it owns, the adaptation surfaces it may change, the outcome oracle, and the comparison that would justify retaining a learned improvement. Then implement that journey using the smallest adequate combination of existing mechanisms. Advance the substrate when the resulting evidence identifies an organizational limitation. This keeps the user focused on direction and semantic judgments while the assistant takes responsibility for the ordinary work, including its own tool management. The same facilities can subsequently support research-to-experiment-to-artifact work; software construction is the immediate target, not the limit of the larger ambition.

- Keep research inputs, executable artifacts, reproduction conditions and uncertainty attributable. Bounded investigation does not make open-ended evaluation an oracle.
- Later research gets a source-linked disposition and may revise a proposed choice; it does not automatically supersede an accepted owner baseline.

## 3. Explicit Evidentiary Limits and Gaps

1. **Absence of Experimental Controls:** None of the examined sources present randomized controlled experiments or statistically verified productivity metrics comparing alternative governance workflows. They represent institutional codifications, practitioner experience reports, or pedagogical consensus.
2. **Organizational Scale Asymmetry:** Practices from Google (SRC-3), Rust (SRC-2), and Kubernetes (SRC-4) reflect hyper-scaled, highly distributed, or corporate multi-stakeholder environments. Directly transplanting their multi-layer triage or formal gating into a 2–5 person research/engineering team risks administrative drag without delivering proportional safety.
3. **Exploratory Research vs. Production Systems:** Wilson et al. (SRC-5) advises that imposing heavyweight industrial software engineering practices on early-stage exploratory research carries a risk of tool fatigue and abandonment, advocating good-enough practices scaled to team maturity.

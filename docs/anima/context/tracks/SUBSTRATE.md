**Current source/design home:** `substrate/`. **Status:** proposal and research direction; no selected permanent host, qualified ANIMA runtime, or source migration.

## User ambition and scope

The user directly corrects a relationship-middleware-only interpretation. The desired system should let a substrate accommodate modules and worlds, join them without freezing every relationship as an API, change boundaries and responsibility, and support integration, assimilation, merging, splitting, temporary or persistent coalitions, reusable composites, and later evolution. Worlds may host worlds; organization may adapt to environmental change and use existing primitives to form a use-specific capability. The intended collective is not confined to one human and one AI: the user points to multiple people, AI systems, tools, computational worlds, and eventually physical systems. The user wants specialists to resolve local details under higher-level obligations so central context and attention stay bounded.

The detailed A2 holon/membrane/coalition proposal and A1/A3/A4/B podcast/model passages are preserved as supplied inspiration with mixed authorship. They are not a ratified five-layer architecture, universal ontology, or result. Preserve the mechanisms as hypotheses and evaluate actual changes in state, ownership, responsibility, lifecycle, or internal machinery followed by reuse.

## Existing foundations and naming boundary

The broad top-level `substrate/` directory contains a design proposal and world-boundary/research documents. Cordis and DSH provide candidate composition, lifecycle, and execution foundations; the proposal argues they do not by themselves establish persistent adaptive relationships, organizational transformations, or developmental capability. These are inspected/reported foundations, not a qualified substrate.

STC also contains a package named `substrate`, whose current role is mechanical quality-gate execution and receipt verification. It must remain separately identified. The two folders do not share an owner or runtime merely because of the name.

## Open implementation space and evidence target

The main host alternatives—extend DSH; use Cordis with DSH at an appropriate runtime/interface seam; or develop a distinct host with adapters—remain proposals. No choice is selected. Small versioned exports can help independent work now; this does not settle every future relationship or imply the system is limited to fixed adapters. The user's latest direct extension includes software that can adapt to environmental changes, can be trained for a use case, and can compose or mutate existing primitives. A reusable process for proposing, assessing, retaining and inheriting such changes is a candidate research direction; the assistant's detailed cycle at main-session L1619 is not yet user-approved architecture.

The active programme proposes W0–W3: choose a tentative host and two meaningfully different environments; demonstrate nested hosting and local responsibilities; show revisable cooperation across representations; then demonstrate a real state/ownership/responsibility/lifecycle/internal change and subsequent composite reuse against a straightforward composition baseline, with cost. These nodes remain `planned`. A folder hierarchy, message exchange, wrapper, or changed API alone is not structural integration evidence.

## Research position

The user's preferred bet map is A → C → B → D, with many intermediate steps before D and room for earlier enabling hardware work. The user prefers substantial foundations before stronger cumulative-discovery/RSI-adjacent work, while welcoming intelligence and improvement now. Preserve both the longer-term evolutionary ambition and the staging; neither becomes a blanket gate over independent useful delivery.

# Reusable procedures

Store versioned workflows, scripts, skills, and construction recipes here once they have explicit applicability conditions, prerequisites, exceptions, dependencies, execution/check instructions, provenance, and supporting episodes.

The user's debate/frame → epistemic foraging → architecture → delegated construction → independent audit → integration/follow-up process is a supplied seed recorded in `context/tracks/STC.md`. It is task-dependent and may be shortened, reordered, corrected, or replaced when evidence supports that choice. Its mention here does not claim a qualified learner or a universal workflow.

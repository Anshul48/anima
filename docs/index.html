The Wrong Total

Maya runs a neighborhood bakery. One morning the online shop rings a $14 order up as $41. She has until noon. Here is how the fix travels the map — each stop is one helper, explored below.

1 · Remember

The memory map replays how the total was built; the whispering librarian warns Maya she tried this shortcut before.

Meet the librarian

2 · Argue

Two candidate fixes debate against strict checks. The weaker one loses in the open; the survivor becomes the fix.

Watch them argue

3 · Build

The work lives in a saved workspace. The laptop dies mid-fix — reopen, every byte intact — then merge with a teammate.

Survive the crash

4 · Check

Four automatic checks pass and a signed proof is issued. One tampered byte, and the inspector seals it off.

Demand the receipt

5 · Act VISION

One day, the fix clicks through the shop itself and verifies the total. Drawn, not built — an early seed today.

See the seed

The five helpers

Every helper page follows one grammar: what it does, one analogy, one demonstration, one future idea. Legend: REAL-REPLAY you can re-run it · RECEIPT measured and dated · ILLUSTRATED drawn concept · SYNTHETIC stand-in for private material.

REAL-REPLAY

Substrate — the Build stop

A notebook that survives a house fire: force-quit everything, reopen, every word intact.

Deep page: the crash, the diff, the merge

REAL-REPLAY RECEIPT

SST — the Argue stop

A debate club where the loser stays on stage — you trust the winner because you watched.

Deep page: the debate, the corpse, the winner

RECEIPT

STC — the Check stop

A building inspector who signs only what passes. No signature, no merge.

Deep page: the gates, the proof, the seal-break

ILLUSTRATED

Entorhinal — the Remember stop

A librarian who whispers, never touches: briefs you, then shreds the note.

Deep page: the warning, the shredding, the audit

ILLUSTRATED-ONLY

Reflex — the Act stop (seed)

A reflex, not an essay: catch the glass before you think. Early build — ambition drawn, not shipped.

Deep page: the ladder, the real chip, the future

Honest limits

  • This map runs nothing itself — each helper is a separate project you install and run.
  • Ambition is always labeled VISION (drawn, not built); measurements carry RECEIPT (re-check them yourself).
  • No private data appears here: demonstrations use rehearsal stand-ins, never real user traces.

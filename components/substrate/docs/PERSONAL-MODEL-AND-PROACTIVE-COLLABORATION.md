**Personal models and proactive collaboration — 2026-10-03**

Status: the user's ambition, with proposed conceptual distinctions for future builds. No personal-model integration, activity collection, or autonomous execution was implemented in this discussion. Entorhinal was inspected only at the level of its directory and high-level build description; its operational completeness was not assessed.

The user wants AI to progressively carry more of the work: make associations, initiate useful investigation, prepare insights, recognize where the user's contribution would change the direction, ask consequential clarifications before expensive dead ends, and learn better collaboration patterns over time. Entorhinal is the intended source of local contextual understanding, with TRACE supplementing it later. The user's direction takes precedence over the accompanying weaker-model podcast.

The research target is an ecosystem of people, AI systems, tools, and computational worlds whose way of working improves through experience. The user's 2026-10-03 correction explicitly rejects confining this to one person and one AI. A personal digital twin is one ingredient. The larger target includes learning how to help people, share work across heterogeneous participants, and turn useful episodes of joint reasoning into capabilities that transfer to later problems.

**What the system needs to model**

- The person: stated goals and preferences, relevant knowledge, distinctive contributions, current context, and uncertain estimates of available attention or readiness.
- Its own capabilities: what it can investigate, construct, verify, and retain; where its confidence has been misleading; and which other participants can help.
- The problem: the alternatives, missing information, bottlenecks, relevant evidence, and consequences of selecting a branch.
- The collaboration: which questions, explanations, independent actions, representations, and divisions of work produced useful outcomes, including their costs and failures.

These are complementary views, not a prescribed four-module architecture. A personal model alone cannot determine whether a question or action will help without understanding the task and the system's own competence.

Distinguish observed habits, explicitly expressed preferences, temporary circumstances, and goals for change. They can disagree. Behaviour is evidence about the person, not a binding instruction to reproduce their current routines. User corrections and current direction can revise earlier interpretations. A digital twin remains partial and uncertain.

**How initiative can develop**

The system can notice connections before being asked, relate new material to active research questions, prepare alternative formulations, investigate a cheap discriminating check, and perform work covered by an existing mandate. It can surface a prepared suggestion when it is useful, or ask for direction when an unresolved distinction would materially change the work. It should also learn when to continue quietly.

Proactivity is evaluated through outcomes. More suggestions, more activity, or more interruptions do not themselves establish better assistance. The relevant question is whether these interventions produce better insights and verified progress with less wasted exploration, rework, and attention cost.

For clarification, use a bounded approximation to the expected improvement in the consequential decision or avoided work, compared with the cost of interrupting the user. High uncertainty alone is insufficient. Some research should proceed independently while a question about another branch remains pending. Existing explicit scope and authorization continue to govern action; an inferred preference does not create new authority.

The user's valuable contributions are changing rather than confined permanently to an oracle role. An early intervention may supply a missing research distinction, analogy, evaluation criterion, or formulation. Retaining that intervention can allow the system to handle analogous future cases, while the user contributes to a more difficult frontier. Transfer needs evidence beyond replaying the original situation or copying the user's phrasing.

**An illustrative research episode**

New material suggests a geometric or causal representation. The system independently connects it to the user's schema-plus-delta and developmental-boundary ambitions, checks the relevant primary sources, and separates plausible mechanisms from speculative claims. It prepares competing formulations and a small comparison that could change the next architectural decision. Before committing expensive work, it identifies the missing piece of direction with the greatest likely effect and asks for that piece. Within the established mandate it then carries out the investigation, retains the outcome and costs, and revises its future collaboration policy.

This is a desired behaviour, not a demonstrated current capability. Its value would come from improving across subsequent episodes, including unfamiliar ones, rather than requiring the user to reconstruct the same context and judgment repeatedly.

**Where this belongs in the existing map**

Entorhinal supplies contextual observations and local hypotheses about the person. TRACE can relate episodes, preferences, sources, conceptual associations, and retained procedures. SST can explore formulations and alternatives; STC and other execution systems can carry out and verify work. These are proposed roles consistent with the discussion, not claims of an integrated path today.

The substrate supports persistent state, heterogeneous participants, relationships, and changes in organization. Collaboration policies can influence routing, representation exchange, and division of work while respecting world responsibilities. The personal model need not become the substrate itself or require every world to share one ontology. This includes multiple people and AI systems, domain engineers, tools, and physical automation described in the [hardware roadmap](HARDWARE-AND-RESEARCH-ACCELERATION.md). The user is an important participant within a broader developing ecosystem.

This is a cross-cutting ambition within A → C → B → D. Retained experience becomes usable knowledge under A; repeatable collaboration strategies can become schemas and operations under C; discovery of better strategies and demonstrated transfer contributes to B. Early useful learning can start before the fuller ecosystem exists.

**Learning from a changing relationship**

The AI affects the activity it later observes. A workflow chosen after its recommendation is partly an outcome of that recommendation. Silence or acceptance does not by itself prove a durable preference, correctness, or independent support for an association. Keep user statements, observed activity, AI proposals, and verified outcomes attributable to their origins.

Assess fresh connections, successful branch choices, verified completion, rework, user effort, and retained advantage on later tasks. Preserve alternative formulations and useful source diversity, as discussed for vector 13. Optimization for agreement or familiar wording could otherwise suppress the surprises that make the collaboration valuable.

**Research anchors and limits**

[LLM Agents Grounded in Self-Reports Enable General-Purpose Simulation of Individuals](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.10109) studies predictions of held-out survey and behavioural outcomes using interview and survey data. It supports investigating useful personal models, while leaving this research collaboration ambition unproven.

[Proactive Agent](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.12361) studies proactive assistance proposals based on activity observations and human labels. Predicting welcome assistance is a narrower target than independently completing useful research work.

[Value of Information: A Framework for Human-Agent Communication](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.06407) studies deciding whether to ask by weighing expected utility gain against user cognitive cost. It is a relevant mechanism to investigate, not a complete solution to long-horizon collaboration or creative discovery.

The podcast's geometry, diffusion, graph-constrained generation, and causal-evaluation ideas remain candidate mechanisms for specific problems. Its narrative does not establish that one stack is necessary, that a graph guarantees factual truth, or that cerebral computation follows the proposed mathematics.
